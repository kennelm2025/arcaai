* **R1 — Use-case register.** Canonical register: 11-use-case catalogue (Banking Architecture table), 3 in Phase 1 build scope (Fraud, Compliance, RM), 9 by end of Phase 2. AML is not a separate catalogue entry; it sits in the Fraud vertical roadmap. *Applied: BA, SA, EB, deck.*
* **R2 — Layer models.** Banking Architecture L1–L5 is the canonical technical model. Learning Bank's five-layer set renamed "five capabilities" (a value lens) with an explicit mapping to L1–L5. *Applied: LB §4.*
* **R3 — Positioning.** Client-facing strategic material leads with "the AI control layer for regulated banking decisions"; engineering documents keep "platform" internally but carry the control-layer strapline. *Applied: BA cover, deck slides 1 \& 10.*

* **R5 — Vector store.** Phase 2 vector store is a self-managed on-prem OpenSearch cluster. No managed-cloud vector store: rm\_knowledge contains customer data. *Applied: SA, TI.*
* **R6 — Jurisdiction \& currency.** UK-first (PRA/FCA primary; EBA/Basel as supranational context); GBP throughout. Cost model restated at €0.86/£, June 2026. *Applied: SA, TI.*
* **R7 — Latency ladder.** ML scoring P99 <200 ms; retrieval <100 ms; full conversational query typically 5–10 s, SLA <15 s; API synchronous SLA <10 s. The "one to two seconds" worked-scenario claim and the <3 s full-flow target removed. *Applied: BA.*
* **R8 — Performance targets.** Banking Architecture use-case table is the single canonical target set. Fraud: ROC-AUC >0.85 is the Phase 1 sign-off gate; 0.90 is the indicative target. Phase 1 gates reference the table verbatim. *Applied: BA, TI.*
* **R9 — Vocabulary purge.** tip records → recommendation and outcome records; tip settlement → outcome-matching (closed-loop feedback) service; min edge threshold → high-risk score threshold; SP-equivalent → rules-engine baseline; ROI → named per-vertical business metric (fraud: precision uplift at fixed alert budget; compliance: breach-class recall; RM: rank correlation vs expert rating). *Applied: SA, EB.*
* **R10 — Calibration normalisation.** Sum-to-one (softmax) group normalisation applies only where outcomes within a group are genuinely mutually exclusive. None of the eleven use cases qualifies; calibrated independent probabilities are the deliverable. Blueprint §12 step 5 and Gate G7 corrected. *Applied: EB.*
* **R11 — EU AI Act dates.** High-risk credit-decision obligations restated to the deferred schedule (December 2027, remainder 2028, per the Commission's digital omnibus), caveated "subject to final adoption; verified June 2026". Re-verify at first client use. *Applied: LB.*
* **R12 — Knowledge-base scale.** 60K+ chunks is the Phase 1 (three-collection) basis; deck restated from 120K+ to 60K+. 120K+ may be cited only as labelled nine-vertical target state. *Applied: deck slide 1.*
* **R13 — Document map / sister document.** Engineering Blueprint retitled "Production Engineering Blueprint — ML Pipeline Sister Document"; Banking Architecture's sister-document references now name it. * **DEC-0011 (2026-07-25) — B7 seed corpus is synthetic, with a named open-licence subset (closed, July 2026).** Discharges the B7 entry criterion requiring a corpus sourcing decision before any ingest (RAT-01 §5, B7). The B7 RAG corpus will not contain FCA Handbook, JMLSG, UK Finance or other restrictively licensed third-party text, in the repo or in any local vector index. Basis: the FCA Handbook terms prohibit including site content in any public **or private** electronic retrieval system or service without prior written permission, require a licence agreement for reproduction, and separately prohibit commercial use without a licence; permitted use is limited to printing and downloading extracts for personal use or use within an individual firm. A ChromaDB index is a private electronic retrieval system on the plain reading. JMLSG terms permit download for personal or legitimate internal business purposes but prohibit distribution to third parties, circulation for profit, and transmission to any other person; a client demonstration surfacing retrieved chunks is not internal business use. Rejected — local-only ingest outside the repo: keeping third-party text out of git addresses publication but not the prohibition on inclusion in a private retrieval system, because the local index is itself the restricted artefact. Recorded as considered and rejected because it is the intuitive answer and will be proposed again. Adopted: (a) a synthetic seed corpus of 50+ documents authored for the build, mirroring the structure and register of UK fraud and financial-crime guidance without reproducing any of it; (b) a small named subset of genuinely open material — sources under the Open Government Licence, including FCA outputs where the OGL is expressly stated to apply, legislation.gov.uk, and HM Treasury published material — to prove the ingest path handles real documents with real structure. The corpus manifest licence column therefore carries two values only: `synthetic-arcaai` and `OGL-v3.0`. Consequences: ground truth is controlled, so the RAGAS eval set and the B7 negative test are constructible rather than found; and the B7 entry criterion moves from externally dependent to self-resolvable (RAT-01 §3.2), removing the longest pole from the B7 entry path. Forward position: licensed regulatory content in a client deployment is either covered by the client’s own permissions as a regulated firm, or requires a licence negotiated by ArcaAI at commercialisation — a commercial/B12 item, not a B7 item. Limitation: coordinator’s reading of published terms, not legal advice. The FCA drafting expresses the retrieval-system prohibition as subject to the personal/firm-use permission, which a lawyer might read more permissively; the synthetic route is adopted partly so that this ambiguity never has to be resolved. If licensed content is ever proposed for the corpus, that is the point at which a solicitor’s opinion is obtained, and no earlier.

* **DEC-0012 (2026-07-25) — Two SS1/23 mapping gaps adopted: generated model inventory at B8, `adjustment_event` at B9 (closed, July 2026).** Source: WS-D item 4, SS1/23 principle mapping for the B8/B9 artefact set (`docs/governance/SS1-23_PRINCIPLE_MAPPING.md`, ratified 25 Jul 2026; reviewed by ChatGPT and Grok, both concur). Each is a scope addition to a stage defined in the locked Build & Quality Plan and therefore requires a DEC on the same basis `outcome_event` did under DEC-0010. **Gap 1 — generated model inventory, B8 exit, record-only.** SS1/23 Principle 1 expects a comprehensive model inventory; the CL-12 provenance manifest is per-artefact and generated at build, and is not one. A generated table is produced at B8 exit from inputs that already exist — the provenance manifest and the RAT-07 tiering record — carrying model_id, version, artefact_sha256, tier, lifecycle_state, last_validated and dependencies. Lifecycle state is included from the outset (active / deprecated / retired / withdrawn / archived): an inventory without retirement states is wrong the first time a model is replaced, and this is the same discipline as B7 time-versioned corpus eligibility. Explicitly NOT a maintained register with owners or approval workflow — that is bank-side. Record-only; not gate-blocking at B8. **Gap 2 — `adjustment_event` contract and append-only table, non-deferrable B9 exit item.** The architecture records what the system decided and what the outcome was, but has nowhere to record that a person overrode the decision and why. One artefact closes three things: SS1/23 Principle 5 (expert judgement applied to model output must be justified and recorded), UK GDPR Article 22C (right to human review of significant automated decisions, per CL-21), and the Learning Bank claim that decisions are overridable with the override in the audit trail. Shape only: correlation_id, original_decision, adjusted_decision, actor_id, actor_role, justification, timestamp; append-only, joinable to the run on correlation id. `actor_role` is a controlled vocabulary (system / reviewer / approver / override_authority), not a free field — banks distinguish who reviewed from who approved, and under SM&CR accountability attaches to a named individual in a named role. Human-override UI and workflow remain deferred. **Scope note.** Both gaps are recorded here; the obligations are folded into `B8_GATE.md` and `B9_GATE.md` at their entry, and the mapping document is superseded once they exist. The SS1/23 firm-scope precision and the Principle 4 claim-discipline statement are carried to the next Banking Architecture revision with the CL-17/19/20 bundle.

* **DEC-0014 (2026-07-28) — Corpus manifest is a Git-versioned governed artefact with a single audit snapshot table (closed, July 2026).** Resolves the design question behind the first B7 required exit item (B7_GATE §3): where the corpus manifest lives and what `audit_run.corpus_version` — a column existing in a ratified, built, immutable schema — refers to, which until this decision was nothing. Reviewed by ChatGPT (approve with amendments, all adopted) and Grok (approve); the one material split (Q4, hash scope) was resolved by adopting ChatGPT's two-hash design, which satisfies Grok's underlying requirement — see below. **Adopted:** (1) `verticals/fraud/corpus/MANIFEST.yaml` is the sole governing artefact for corpus identity, licence, SA5 classification and eligibility. Vertical-side deliberately: corpus content is business semantics under ADR-0009; loader machinery (schema validation, hashing, snapshot insert) is platform-side. Document fields are split **identity** (id, source, licence per the DEC-0011 vocabulary `synthetic-arcaai` | `OGL-v3.0`, SA5 classification, content sha256, eligibility history) — immutable — versus **processing observations** (chunker_version, embedding_model, chunk_count, ingest_timestamp) — updatable with a manifest version change, never touching identity. Eligibility is a list of dated transitions (state ∈ included | pending_review | withdrawn | deprecated, date, reason); a mutable flag is prohibited. (2) One append-only runtime table `corpus_version` under the existing grant pattern: version_id (PK), human label `manifest_version` (ergonomic only, ignored for identity), `manifest_sha256`, `eligible_set_sha256`, `retrieval_snapshot_sha256`, `eligible_doc_count` (**derived metadata, cached for convenience — the manifest is authoritative and this value is never treated as truth**), `loaded_at`; CHECK constraints per the NULL-not-sentinel discipline. `audit_run.corpus_version` stores version_id. The snapshot row is evidence that a version was loaded; it is **not** a reconstruction of the manifest and must never be used to regenerate it. (3) **Two hashes, two questions** (panel Q4 resolution): `eligible_set_sha256` over the sorted eligible (doc_id, content_sha256) pairs answers "did these runs use the same documents"; `retrieval_snapshot_sha256` over the sorted (doc_id, content_sha256, chunker_version, embedding_model, chunk_count) tuples answers "did these runs retrieve from an operationally identical corpus". Grok's requirement that reproducibility be sensitive to chunking is met by the second hash; conflating both into one hash weakens both questions. (4) **Canonicalisation is normative:** deterministic UTF-8 serialisation with stable key ordering, LF endings, insignificant whitespace removed, before hashing — underspecified canonicalisation means two loaders can hash one manifest differently. (5) **Append-only is mechanical at load, not conventional:** against the previously loaded version of the manifest, the loader hard-fails on deleted transition entries, modified historical entries, or reordered transition history; appending a later transition is the only valid change to eligibility. (6) **No `git_commit` column** — considered and rejected (unanimous): a manifest's identity is its content hash; the commit is how a human finds the content, not what it is; recording it creates a second identity and couples replay to rewritable history. Replay chain: audit_run → corpus_version → manifest_sha256 → content-addressed lookup in the repo; `git log -S` locates the commit when a human wants context. (7) **Hash-pinning is the load-bearing tamper-evidence mechanism:** git history is append-only by policy, not mechanism (no branch protection; CL-E1 records a live force-push). At every load the loader recomputes `manifest_sha256` against the pinned row — mismatch is a hard failure. A scheduled ci-mlops step additionally re-hashes previously loaded versions (ChatGPT: load catches corruption before execution, CI before release; adopted now on cost grounds rather than deferred to Grok's external-contribution trigger, which instead becomes the point at which CL-E1's branch protection — prevention, not detection — is mandatory). Postgres is thereby tamper-evidence for git without becoming a second source of truth. **Rejected:** YAML-only (no queryable replay referent; transitions unenforced); four runtime tables `corpus_document`/`corpus_ingest_event`/`corpus_eligibility_transition`/`corpus_version` (governed configuration duplicated into SQL creates a second change-control system whose divergence from the repo is nobody's job to notice — the design needed a reconciliation export on day one, the tell that it was two systems; recorded because it is a natural elaboration of the correct audit-table pattern and will be proposed again); Postgres-as-master with generated YAML (inverts change control: the reviewed artefact would derive from unreviewed rows). **Consequences:** first B7 build items are the manifest schema + validator, seed manifest per DEC-0011, loader with snapshot insert and the mechanical append-only check, `corpus_version` model + grants + denial tests, and the ci-mlops re-hash step; the conftest GRANT_SQL duplication (2026-07-27 handover observation) is fixed in the same PR by executing `sql/governance_grants.sql` rather than a copy; CL-E1 rises from housekeeping to design dependency with a named trigger (first external contributor or client engagement → branch protection mandatory). **Standing constraint:** if the loader ever writes eligibility state into Postgres, or a convenience table reintroduces corpus state outside the manifest, the hybrid collapses into the rejected four-table anti-pattern — the constraint is stated in the loader contract and the schema docstring. Related: DEC-0011, DEC-0013, ADR-0009, ADR-0010, CL-E1, CF-1/B7-b, B7_GATE §3.
