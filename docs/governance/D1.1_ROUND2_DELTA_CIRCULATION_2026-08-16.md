## 3. The window

**Duration: 24 hours.** The window opens at the operator's send time and the
deadline is that time plus 24 hours.

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Send time, UTC | `________________` — filled by the operator at send |
| Deadline, UTC | send time plus 24 hours |

**The slot is deliberately empty at authoring.** A window whose start is stated
before the send has happened is a prediction, and a deadline computed from a
predicted start is not a deadline. **No circulation act may proceed with this slot
unfilled**, because the reviewers cannot be told a deadline that does not yet
exist.

**Non-response is recorded as non-response.** A reviewer who does not return within
the window has that recorded in the delta disposition by name, and the chair
adjudicates their findings explicitly, on the record, with reasons. **Silence is
not assent**, and a finding adjudicated as discharged in a reviewer's absence is
marked as chair-adjudicated-unverified rather than reported as a reviewer's
DISCHARGED.

## 5. The verification question, and its exact form

For each finding assigned to you at §6, answer this and nothing else:

> **Does Rev C's implementing text discharge this finding — that is, does it close
> the defect the finding identified, as the chair ruled it?**
>
> **DISCHARGED** or **NOT DISCHARGED**, per finding. If NOT DISCHARGED, one
> paragraph of reasons.

### 5.1 What the test is, stated precisely because two findings turn on it

**The test is whether the chair's adopted remedy closes the defect your finding
identified.** It is not whether your proposed remedy was implemented. Two of the
findings below were sustained with the remedy partly adopted or not adopted as
proposed, each with reasons on the record:

- `F-GEM-REG-01` — the proposed hard gate on Regime 2 execution was **not**
  adopted; an external-citation bar was adopted instead. Reasons at disposition
  R2-2, three of them, recorded in full.
- `F-GEM-REG-03` — the `invalidation_status` field and mandatory filtering were
  adopted; the **cryptographic revocation signature was not**. Reasons at
  disposition R2-7.

For those two, the question is whether the adopted remedy closes the exposure, not
whether the chair chose the remedy you would have chosen.

### 5.2 The difference between NOT DISCHARGED and a dissent

Both are admissible and they are recorded differently, so the distinction is worth
one sentence each.

- **NOT DISCHARGED** — the adopted fix, read against Rev C's actual text, fails to
  close the substance of your finding. This is a verification outcome and it
  prevents acceptance.
- **DISSENT** — the substance is closed, and you disagree with the chair's choice
  of remedy, or with a severity adjudication. This returns **verbatim**, is
  disposed on its own row, and is never summarised. It does not convert into a
  finding in this round.

Say which you are doing. A response that is a dissent phrased as NOT DISCHARGED,
or the reverse, is adjudicated as written rather than as inferred.

### 5.3 Scope limits, all four of them

1. **No whole-document re-review.** Read the implementing sections named in your
   scope table and the surrounding text you need to judge them.
2. **No new findings**, with one exception: **a defect IN a fix itself is
   admissible**, is rated by you, and is dispositioned on the record. That
   exception exists because a fix that introduces a new defect has not discharged
   anything.
3. **MINORs are not reopened.** All eleven were disposed at the round-2
   disposition; their Rev C landings are traceable at Appendix C.2 of the plan.
4. **Round-1 carriage is not reopened.** All four of you confirmed it faithful at
   round 2, one by cross-checking all 35 row IDs.

residual MATERIAL returns DISCHARGED, or is chair-adjudicated as discharged where
unverified, the round returns no sustained BLOCKING or MATERIAL and Rev C is
ACCEPTED.** The two limbs of the acceptance stop are already satisfied in

**What can still prevent acceptance**, stated so the round has a real negative
outcome and not only a nominal one: any NOT DISCHARGED on a residual MATERIAL, and
any admissible defect-in-a-fix rated BLOCKING or MATERIAL by its reviewer and
