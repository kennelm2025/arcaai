## Reading notes, outside the ruling

These do not add to the ruling and are recorded so a later reader does not
have to reconstruct them.

**The lapse is scoped, not wholesale.** What lapses is the basis-stale
portion — the original guard-family F2-gap coverage, whose basis closed on
19 August 2026. The reason a wholesale lapse was not ruled is stated at
Item 2b: it would break load-bearing live machinery that was never intended
as collateral.

**Two clauses survive the lapse, on different footings, and the difference
matters.**

- **A4.5**, the outcome-file convention, survives as a **free-standing rule,
  re-based** on the queue protocol / DEC-0019. It no longer rests on AMD-A4's
  original premise, so a citation of A4.5 should name the queue protocol as
  its authority rather than the lapsed basis. This re-basing is the authority
  under which the return-leg append in the PROMPT 154 grant manifest is
  performed.
- **A4.6**, the autonomous polling loop, is recorded **as ruled at sitting and
  hedged deliberately**: it is not separately attested in-tree, and the
  attestation is owed at the next convenient transcription. The **unattended
  loop remains WITHHELD per DEC-0019** regardless — the hedge concerns the
  attestation of the clause, not the operational status of the loop, and
  nothing here relaxes the withholding.

**No retrospective rewrite.** Acts performed under the then-live amendment
family on or before 21 August 2026 remain valid. The lapse is effective from
the start of session on 22 August 2026 and runs forward only.

**Standing flexibility principle applies.** Per envelope §0 as incorporated
into the sitting record, this lapse is binding for current direction and is
not irreversible architecture; it may be revisited, narrowed, widened or
reversed at a later sitting on evidence or operating experience.

**Consequence for the harness-instruction conflict, stated so it is not
inferred.** The passage at `CLAUDE.md` lines 106–117 asserted AMD-A4's
win-rule against a contradicting harness instruction as operative law. That
passage is amended in the same act as this note to record the supersession.
The historical statement is preserved there as historical rather than
deleted.
