## Routed questions

### Q1 — Retrieval quality: a plainly-worded statutory question did not retrieve the statute

**Source:** CL-27, finding F1. Full record at
`docs/governance/COMMISSIONING_SESSION_RECORD_2026-08-13_d22a-runner-spike.md`.

Statement, carried in full from the record:

> **F1 — recall_at_k 0.0 on RQA-001. Recorded, explicitly NOT chased.**
> The statute-grounding query, asking which statutory provision creates the
> offence of fraud by false representation and what must be proved, returned
> synthetic typology and data-protection chunks — SYN-TY-02, SYN-DP-01,
> SYN-DP-02 — while the expected grounding document OGL-0001, the Fraud Act
> 2006 s.2 extract, did not appear in the top 5. This is a retrieval-quality
> question **routed to the Test Plan (D1.1)** by the close ruling, not a spike
> defect. Under the D2.0 frame a scenario's own pass/fail is not an exit
> criterion, and per operator standing note the query, spec, chunking and index
> were **not** tuned to produce a match. It is nonetheless the most substantive
> thing the spike surfaced: the corpus holds the statute, and a plainly-worded
> question about it did not retrieve it.

**Conditions of the observation**, so the Test Plan is not written against a
half-remembered result. Corpus snapshot `2026-08-06.7`, manifest sha256
`97ca36dda960ed31c368c1a4acde04b720926bf4e3ee92ae98b95d65cc340b6a`; 16 eligible
documents, 71 indexed chunks; embedding model pinned at
all-MiniLM-L6-v2 under ONNX; chunker para-pack-v1 at a 220-word target and
380-word cap; top_k 5; expected grounding set of exactly one document. OGL-0001
carries a chunk_count of 1, so the statute occupies a single chunk competing
against multi-chunk synthetic documents.

**What the Test Plan has to decide, stated as questions and not as answers:**

- Whether retrieval quality is in scope for D1.1 at all, or belongs to the
  corpus-design process. The two have different owners.
- What a retrieval-class acceptance threshold should be, given that recall at k
  over an expected set of size one is quantised to zero or one and therefore
  carries no gradient. Scenarios with larger expected sets behave differently
  and may need different thresholds.
- Whether single-chunk documents are systematically disadvantaged against
  multi-chunk documents under this embedding and chunker pairing, and if so
  whether that is a corpus-design fact, a chunker parameter question, or a
  retrieval-configuration question.
- Whether a statute-retrieval scenario class is wanted distinct from corpus QA,
  given that legal text and synthetic narrative documents may not be
  commensurable under one similarity measure.
- What the D1/D2 planted-gap ground truth implies here: a query that retrieves
  the wrong documents confidently is a different failure from one that
  correctly abstains, and the gap-detection scoring path already distinguishes
  them.

**Explicitly out of scope for this brief:** diagnosing the cause. The spike was
barred from chasing it and this brief inherits that bar. The question is routed,
not investigated.
