## 2. The acceptance rule

### 2.1 The rule as it now stands

Circulated to reviewers verbatim, in this form:

> **Rev B is ACCEPTED if the round returns no finding rated BLOCKING or MATERIAL
> and sustained by the chair at that severity. Chair severity adjudication is on
> the record with reasons, both directions. On acceptance: all MINOR/OBSERVATION
> findings are disposed at the acceptance stop (owner or noted-no-action);
> dissents answered per convention.**

**Attribution:** chair-stated 2026-08-13; **amended by the chair 2026-08-15.**

### 2.2 What the amendment did

The 2026-08-15 amendment **removed the subject-to clause as spent.** The clause
made acceptance conditional on the separately-owed regulatory pass. That pass is
no longer separately owed: on Mistral's stand-down the regulatory lens was
reassigned to Gemini and folded *inside* round 2 (§3), so the condition is
satisfied by the round's own composition rather than by a later act. A subject-to
clause pointing at an obligation that now runs inside the round would make
acceptance conditional on something the round itself discharges.

### 2.3 The pre-amendment wording, verbatim

The rule as first stated, transcribed verbatim:

> ROUND 2 ACCEPTANCE RULE (chair-stated, in the round-2 pack): RevB
> ACCEPTED if no finding rated BLOCKING/MATERIAL and sustained by chair
> at that severity; chair severity adjudication on the record both
> directions; MINORs disposed at acceptance; subject to the owed
> Mistral pass (MATERIAL+ reopens via §5A:101 amendment, not RevC).

**Source:** `ARCAAI_CHAT_HANDOVER_2026-08-15.md`, lines 87–91. **Chair-stated
2026-08-13.**

*Provenance note, recorded because the citation was corrected in flight.* The
transcription was first directed to a different file, `BOOT_NOTES_2026-08-15.md`
§2, which does not exist — not at the stated path and nowhere under that
directory, confirmed by recursive search on 2026-08-15. The wording was located in
the handover named above and is cited to it. The text was **not** taken from the
instruction that described it, nor reconstructed from its known effect: a quotation
assembled from what a rule was understood to do, placed in the section that exists
to make the rule's history legible, would be the exact failure this pack guards
against.

### 2.4 What lapsed with the subject-to clause, and why

The removed clause carried a **reopening mechanism** as well as a condition:
`MATERIAL+ reopens via §5A:101 amendment, not RevC`. Recorded explicitly because
it is a provision rather than a qualifier, and it should be seen to lapse
deliberately rather than incidentally.

**Chair rationale, on the record:** the §5A:101 reopening mechanism existed only
because the regulatory pass would have arrived *after* acceptance. With the lens
inside round 2, a regulatory finding rated MATERIAL or above is simply a round-2
finding, blocking acceptance directly under the main limb of §2.1. **The mechanism
lapses as unnecessary, not as discarded** — the outcome it existed to produce is
now produced by the rule's ordinary operation.

## 4. Gemini's brief — the regulatory lens

**Primary brief for round 2.** Gemini reads D1.1 Rev B as the panel's regulatory
reviewer. The other three members read to their round-1 briefs.

### 4.1 Scope — the five frameworks

The lens is:

- **EU AI Act**
- **UK GDPR**
- **PRA SS1/23** (model risk management)
- **FCA** (conduct and operational expectations)
- **DORA** (digital operational resilience)

*Provenance note: this enumeration is taken verbatim from the operator's
regulatory-seat ruling of 2026-08-15, which names all five. It is not an
inference about what the lens should cover.*

### 4.2 What the brief asks

Rev B is a **test plan**: it governs how evidence about a retrieval system is
produced, admitted and invalidated. The regulatory question is therefore not
whether the system complies, but **whether the evidence this plan produces would
survive a skilled person's examination** — and whether the plan claims more, or
less, than its mechanisms support.

The chair does not narrow the lens beyond that framing. Round 1's findings arrived
in clusters the reviewers chose for themselves, and pre-sorting a reviewer's
attention is how a panel is turned into a confirmation exercise.

### 4.3 Two features of the plan the lens will meet early

Named because they are unusual, not to steer the finding:

- **The two-regime model.** Regime 1 (commissioning) results are inadmissible as
  gate evidence **permanently**, not merely until the plan is ruled, and cannot be
  promoted retroactively. Regime 2 (formal execution) requires this plan ruled
  ACCEPTED.
- **The PROVISIONAL coverage label** at §2.4. The plan states in terms that a
  Regime 2 run against the current corpus is **not** evidence of fraud-typology
  coverage, because the manifest carries no typology field.

### 4.4 Standing constraint on all reviewers

Panel members act as **interrogators, never authors**. Reviewer output is
concurrence, recommendation and finding — never a ruling. All rulings are the
operator's. A reviewer phrasing an outcome as a ruling has it recorded as a
recommendation, as happened at the TOR panel pass.
