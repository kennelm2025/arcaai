## Boundary tag vocabulary (applies to every new ADR and DEC entry)

- `engine-side` — internal to the technical AI engine; no vertical may depend on it.
- `app-side` — internal to a vertical; the engine makes no assumption about it.
- `boundary` — touches what a vertical may see, call, or assume of the engine.
