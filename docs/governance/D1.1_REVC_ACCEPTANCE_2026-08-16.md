## 5. The Gemini exchange, recorded because the trail should show why

Gemini's return states in its header, and it is repeated here rather than left in
one file:

> the reviewer's first two responses were document summaries containing no
> verdicts and were rejected as non-responsive. This return was produced on the
> third prompt, a structured verdict-template prompt naming the prior failure.
> The verdicts below are the reviewer's own, produced against the implementing
> text; the two rejected summaries were not landed and are not part of the
> round's evidence.

**Why this is recorded in the acceptance instrument.** The delta pack's
non-response machinery exists for a reviewer who does not return; it was not
needed here, and a record that said only "full return, four of four" would be
true and would hide that the fourth return took three attempts. Two things follow
and both are stated rather than implied:

- **The rejected summaries were rejected on form, not on content.** They carried
  no verdicts. Rejecting a non-responsive return and re-prompting is not the same
  act as re-prompting until a reviewer gives a wanted answer, and the distinction
  matters because only one of those is legitimate. The third prompt supplied a
  verdict template and named the prior failure; it did not argue for any verdict.
- **They are not part of the round's evidence** and were not landed. The trail
  records that they existed and why they were set aside.

**A related return-condition gap, recorded honestly.** The delta pack §9 requires
every response to state the verified hash verbatim. Grok stated it; ChatGPT
independently recomputed it and stated it; DeepSeek stated it with an explicit
verification sentence. **Gemini's return did not restate the hash**, and its
coordinator header records that fact in terms. The return is pinned by the pack
and by its header rather than by the reviewer's own statement. This is a
procedural gap in one return, not a defect in a verdict, and it is recorded
because a return condition that goes unmet without being noticed is how return
conditions stop meaning anything.
