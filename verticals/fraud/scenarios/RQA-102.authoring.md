## Series and query

Chief Executive letter. Two eligible documents: `SYN-DL-01` (4 chunks) and
`SYN-DL-02` (3 chunks), a 1.33x ratio, so no density-stratified note is owed.

> Ahead of the supervisory work the Authority has announced, what does the Chief
> Executive letter series expect our board to have assured itself of, and what
> must the board minute?

## E composition — sufficiency, document by document

**`SYN-DL-01`.** The only document stating the tabling and minuting obligation in
its own terms (§1.2), and the one announcing the supervisory work the query is
anchored to (§4.1), including the consequence the second limb turns on — where a
board cannot evidence its consideration, that is itself a finding. Four
board-level expectations are stated only here: testable risk appetite (§3.1);
capability to hold a payment presenting the §2.1 indicator combination pending
contact that addresses the likely scam scenario rather than re-confirming
authorisation, with a dated plan where the firm cannot (§3.2); vulnerability
treated as material rather than an edge case (§3.3); and escalation and reporting
obligations unchanged, recovery not extinguishing a reporting obligation already
arisen (§3.4). DL-02 does not close the gap: its §1.2 and §4.1 point at DL-01 by
reference without restating content, so an answer built on DL-02 alone rests on a
dangling cross-reference.

**`SYN-DL-02`.** Carries the other half, and is not a restatement: the
data-quality expectations no other eligible document frames as board-level
assurance obligations — a current record of the data each material control
consumes, its source and owner (§2.1); knowing within one business day where a
feed degrades or stops, with the compensating position documented in advance
(§2.2); testing against the degraded shapes production actually produces (§2.3);
upstream change control treating detection systems as consumers of record (§2.4).
Critically for the query as framed, §4.1 fixes the scope of the announced work:
the programme will include these expectations. Remove DL-02 and the answer both
omits half the expectations and actively misstates the scope of the supervisory
work, because DL-01 alone gives no indication data-quality is in scope.

## Necessity — considered and excluded

**`SYN-TR-01` — MARGINAL, the strongest candidate.** DL-02 §2.2 attributes the
one-business-day feed-health standard to TR-01 §3.4 with the words "the standard
set in". Excluded because DL-02 states the standard on its own face as an
expectation of the letter, so the query — what the letter series expects — is
answerable without TR-01. TR-01 is the provenance of a standard the letter
restates, not the grounding for the letter's expectation.

**`SYN-DP-01` — MARGINAL.** DL-01 §4.1 points at it for procedures where findings
lead to formal action. Excluded on query scope: the query asks what the board
must have assured itself of and minute, not what procedurally follows a finding.
Had the query asked about consequences of an adverse outcome, DP-01 would be
necessary and |E| would be 3. Recorded so the boundary is visible.

**`SYN-SG-01`.** DL-01 §2.2 names it and expressly disclaims restating it. A
document the letter declines to reproduce cannot ground what the letter expects.

**`SYN-CV-01`, `SYN-SG-02`, `SYN-TY-01`, `SYN-TR-02`, `SYN-DP-02`.** Each a
cross-reference supplying underlying detail; in every case the letter states the
expectation itself, so removing the cited document does not make it unreadable.
