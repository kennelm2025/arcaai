# DP-01 — Decision Procedures: From Supervisory Finding to Formal Action

**Issued by the Authority · Decision Procedures series · Reference DP-01**

## 1. Purpose and status

1.1 This document describes the procedure the Authority follows when
a supervisory finding concerning fraud controls may lead to formal
action against a relevant firm. It exists so that firms know how
decisions about them are made, by whom, and on what material. It is
procedural: nothing here alters the substantive standards in the
Framework or in the guidance and review series (SG-01, SG-02, TR-01,
DL-01).

1.2 The procedure for individual accountability decisions is
addressed separately in DP-02. Where a matter involves both a firm
and individuals, the procedures run in parallel but decisions are
taken separately.

## 2. What the Authority weighs

2.1 The Authority assesses findings against what the firm could
reasonably have known and done at the time, on the records the firm
kept. Contemporaneous records are therefore central. A firm whose
intervention records show the indicators present, the action taken
and the rationale (TR-01 §5.2, SG-01 §6.1) will be assessed on those
records; a firm without such records will be assessed on the absence.

2.2 The Authority distinguishes between control failures and outcome
events. Fraud losses do not of themselves establish a failure; the
question is whether the firm's arrangements were adequate and
operated as designed. Conversely, the absence of loss does not
establish adequacy — an inoperative control that happened not to be
tested by events (TR-01 §3.4 describes silently failed monitoring
rules) is still a finding.

2.3 Escalation and reporting conduct is weighed with particular care.
A firm that met its SG-02 obligations promptly and candidly will find
that weighed in its favour even where the underlying controls failed;
a firm whose reports were late, partial, or drafted to obscure
(SG-02 §4.2) should expect that treated as an aggravating feature
distinct from the underlying failure.

## 3. Stages of the procedure

3.1 **Finding and representation.** A supervisory finding that may
warrant formal action is put to the firm in writing with the material
relied on. The firm has a stated period to make representations, to
correct facts, and to supply records. Decisions not to act on
escalations (SG-02 §2.3) attract specific attention at this stage:
the recorded rationale is either the firm's best evidence or,
where absent, the Authority's.

3.2 **Referral decision.** A decision-maker independent of the
supervision team that made the finding decides whether the matter
proceeds to formal action, closes with remediation, or closes without
action. The decision and its reasons are recorded and communicated to
the firm.

3.3 **Formal action.** Where the matter proceeds, the Authority's
formal powers are exercised through the committee arrangements
described in the Framework, with the firm entitled to make further
representations. The committee sees the firm's representations in
full, not a summary.

3.4 **Publication.** Concluded formal actions are published with
reasons. Thematic and aggregate material (as in TR-01 and the
supervisory work announced in DL-01 §4.2) is published without naming
firms; formal action outcomes name the firm.

## 4. Remediation and undertakings

4.1 At any stage the firm may propose remediation. The Authority
assesses proposals against whether they address the cause rather than
the instance: re-papering a failed control without changing what made
it fail is not remediation. Cohort-level learning of the kind
described in SG-01 §5 is frequently what distinguishes accepted
proposals from rejected ones.

4.2 Where remediation is accepted in place of formal action, it is
recorded as an undertaking with dates, and tested. An undertaking
missed without prior explanation restarts the procedure at 3.2 with
the missed undertaking added to the material.

## 5. Timeliness

5.1 The Authority holds itself to stated periods at each stage and
records where they are exceeded. Firms are entitled to ask where a
matter stands and to receive an answer that reflects the record.

**End of DP-01.**
